*Parametric EQ* boosts or cuts one band of frequencies that you choose.
Use it to bring out the character of a sound, or to remove a frequency that sounds harsh or boomy.

- **FREQ**: the center of the band, from **100** Hz to **12K** (12 kHz).
- **Q**: the width of the band. Turn left for a wide, gentle curve; turn right for a narrow, focused one.
- **LEVEL**: how much the band is boosted (**+**, right) or cut (**-**, left), up to 18 dB either way. In the middle the sound is unchanged.

##### Inputs and outputs

**IN** and **OUT** are the audio input and output.
The module is polyphonic: it processes up to 16 channels.

When you bypass the module in Rack, the input goes straight to the output.

#### Starter tips

To find a harsh or boomy frequency, turn Q to the right, boost LEVEL a lot and sweep FREQ until it jumps out. Then turn LEVEL down below the middle to cut it.

For a gentle tone lift, keep Q to the left and boost LEVEL just a little.

Chain several copies to build a full multi-band EQ.
