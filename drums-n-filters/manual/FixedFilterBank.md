*Fixed Filter Bank* is a 12-band equalizer with a low pass and a high pass filter.
Use it to shape the color of a sound, from subtle tone fixes to the hollow, resonant sound of classic fixed filter banks.

- **125Hz** to **5.6Khz**: twelve knobs, one per band, each one marked with its frequency. Turn a knob right to boost that band, left to cut it, up to 12 dB either way. In the middle the band is flat.
- **LP**: low pass. Removes the highs above its setting, from 12 kHz (fully right, the default) down to about 12 Hz.
- **HP**: high pass. Removes the lows below its setting, from about 12 Hz (fully left, the default) up to 12 kHz.

##### Inputs and outputs

LP and HP each have a CV input.
Each voltage adds to its knob and follows 1V/octave: +1V raises the frequency one octave.

**IN** and **OUT** are the audio input and output.
The module is polyphonic: it filters up to 16 channels, and the LP and HP CVs can be polyphonic too.

When you bypass the module in Rack, the input goes straight to the output.

#### Starter tips

Boost two or three bands next to each other to give a sound a strong, singing color.

Cut the low bands on pads and leads to make room for the bass and the kick.

Patch an LFO into the LP CV input for a slow, breathing filter sweep on top of your EQ settings.

Use LP and HP together to keep only a narrow slice of the sound, like an old telephone or radio.
