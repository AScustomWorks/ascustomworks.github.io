*CMP Kick* plays the bass drum of 8-bit home computer music, with the gritty, lo-fi character of a classic sound chip.
It has two kick sounds, **A** and **B**, and you can blend between them.

- **MORPH**: blends sound A (left) and sound B (right). In the middle you hear both at the same volume. The LED next to MORPH turns red toward A and green toward B.
- **TONE**: shapes the sound. In the middle the sound is unchanged. Turn left to make it darker (fewer highs); turn right to make it thinner (fewer lows).
- **GAIN**: output volume.

##### Inputs and outputs

A trigger at **TRIG** plays the sound from the start.
A new trigger restarts it, even if the last hit is still ringing.

The two CV jacks below GAIN control the knobs: the left one adds to MORPH and the right one adds to TONE.
0V to 10V covers the whole range of each knob; negative voltages turn it down.

**OUT** is the audio output.

#### Starter tips

Patch a sequencer or clock gate into TRIG to start playing.

Patch a slow LFO into the MORPH CV input so each kick in a bar sounds a little different.

Turn TONE left for a round, deep kick; bring it back toward the center for more click.
