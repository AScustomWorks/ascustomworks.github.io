*RCK Toms* plays the toms of the hexagonal electronic drum kits of the 80s, with their punchy, synthetic tone.

The **SOUND** knob picks one of 5 sounds, marked on the panel:

- **HT**: high tom
- **MT**: mid tom
- **LT**: low tom
- **FT**: floor tom 1
- **FT**: floor tom 2

If you change the sound while one is still ringing, the old one fades out quickly instead of cutting off.

- **TONE**: shapes the sound. In the middle the sound is unchanged. Turn left to make it darker (fewer highs); turn right to make it thinner (fewer lows).
- **GAIN**: output volume.

##### Inputs and outputs

A trigger at **TRIG** plays the sound from the start.
A new trigger restarts it, even if the last hit is still ringing.

The **CV** jack above OUT adds to TONE.
0V to 10V covers the whole range of the knob; negative voltages turn it down.

**OUT** is the audio output.

#### Starter tips

Patch a sequencer or clock gate into TRIG to start playing.

Use two or three copies, each on a different tom, for fills across the kit.

Turn TONE left for deep, boomy toms; turn it right for short, tight ones.
